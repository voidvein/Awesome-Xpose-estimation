# Awesome Xpose Estimation

A curated, non-exhaustive index of research on 2D and 3D head, hand and human pose estimation. Historical foundational work and recent methods are included. Publication, preprint, and workshop status are distinguished where known.

[Browse the site](https://voidvein.github.io/Awesome-Xpose-estimation/) · [Sources and curation notes](SOURCES.md)

## Run locally

Serve the repository root with `python -m http.server 8000` and open `http://localhost:8000/`.

## Index

### Head

- **[FaceLift: Semi-supervised 3D Facial Landmark Localization](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Ferman_FaceLift_Semi-supervised_3D_Facial_Landmark_Localization_CVPR_2024_paper.html)** — CVPR 2024 · Published · 3D. 以半监督学习定位 3D 人脸关键点，关注人工定义与 3DMM 注册标注的一致性。
- **[6DoF Head Pose Estimation through Explicit Bidirectional Interaction with Face Geometry](https://www.ecva.net/papers/eccv_2024/papers_ECCV/html/4824_ECCV_2024_paper.php)** — ECCV 2024 · Published · 3D. 通过脸部几何与姿态的双向交互估计 6DoF 头姿，特别关注平移估计。 [Code](https://github.com/asw91666/TRG-Release)
- **[HeadDiff: Exploring Rotation Uncertainty With Diffusion Models for Head Pose Estimation](https://doi.org/10.1109/TIP.2024.3372457)** — IEEE TIP 2024 · Published · 3D. 采用概率扩散回归建模野外图像中的头部旋转不确定性。
- **[On the representation and methodology for wide and short range head pose estimation](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0031320324000141)** — Pattern Recognition 2024 · Published · 3D. 研究不同角度范围的头姿表示和评测，并提供基于 CMU Panoptic 的广角度基准。 [Code](https://github.com/pcr-upm/opal23_headpose)
- **[Infinite 3D Landmarks: Improving Continuous 2D Facial Landmark Detection](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/cgf.15126)** — Computer Graphics Forum 2024 · Published · 2D / 3D. 借助 3D 几何改进连续 2D 人脸关键点检测。
- **[Unsupervised Discovery of Facial Landmarks and Head Pose](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Tourani_Unsupervised_Discovery_of_Facial_Landmarks_and_Head_Pose_CVPR_2025_paper.html)** — CVPR 2025 · Published · 2D / 3D. 使用 Stable Diffusion 特征联合发现人脸关键点与头姿，通过几何约束相互优化。 [Code](https://github.com/touranisatyajit/cvpr2025-uld-headpose)
- **[FaceXFormer: A Unified Transformer for Facial Analysis](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/html/Narayan_FaceXFormer_A_Unified_Transformer_for_Facial_Analysis_ICCV_2025_paper.html)** — ICCV 2025 · Published · 2D / 3D. 统一人脸分析 Transformer，覆盖关键点、头姿、人脸解析及属性等多任务。
- **[Towards unsupervised learning of joint facial landmark detection and head pose estimation](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0031320325000536)** — Pattern Recognition 2025 · Published · 2D / 3D. 通过可学习的标准姿态 3D 关键点与图像重建联合学习关键点和刚体头姿。
- **[Semi-Supervised Unconstrained Head Pose Estimation in the Wild](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.02544)** — arXiv; author reports TPAMI publication 2024 · Preprint · 3D. 利用未标注野外头部图像进行半监督全范围旋转估计。按 arXiv 首发年收录；期刊正式卷期尚待出版社核验。 [Code](https://github.com/hnuzhy/SemiUHPE)
- **[VGGT-HPE: Reframing Head Pose Estimation as Relative Pose Prediction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.10106)** — CVPR Workshops (author-reported) 2026 · Published · 3D. 将绝对头姿回归改写为相对于已知姿态参考图像的几何变换预测；以合成数据微调几何基础模型。
- **[ReVA-HPE: Relative 6DoF Head Pose Estimation with Synthetic Pose Supervision and Personalized Virtual Anchors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03283)** — arXiv; accepted WACV 2027 (author-reported) 2026 · Preprint · 3D. 利用个性化虚拟参考图像进行相对 6DoF 估计，减少对真实参考图像已知姿态的依赖。
- **[STAG-Net: Structure-Aware Attention over Landmark Groups for Lightweight Head Pose Estimation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07828)** — arXiv 2026 · Preprint · 3D. 基于关键点分组和结构注意力进行轻量头姿回归，面向低计算成本部署。
- **[Supervised Descent Method and Its Applications to Face Alignment](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_cvpr_2013/html/Xiong_Supervised_Descent_Method_2013_CVPR_paper.html)** — CVPR 2013 · Published · 2D. 经典级联回归路线：在训练中学习一系列下降方向，测试时无需显式计算 Jacobian 或 Hessian。适合作为理解深度关键点检测之前的优化式对齐方法的入口。
- **[One Millisecond Face Alignment with an Ensemble of Regression Trees](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_cvpr_2014/html/Kazemi_One_Millisecond_Face_2014_CVPR_paper.html)** — CVPR 2014 · Published · 2D. 从稀疏像素强度直接预测人脸关键点，通过梯度提升训练回归树集成。研究速度、缺失标注及数据增强之间的关系，是低成本 CPU 对齐的重要经典。
- **[Face Alignment at 3000 FPS via Regressing Local Binary Features](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_cvpr_2014/html/Ren_Face_Alignment_at_2014_CVPR_paper.html)** — CVPR 2014 · Published · 2D. 利用局部二值特征进行人脸关键点回归，代表高速传统方法路线。标题中的速度为论文实验条件，不应直接与现代 GPU 模型横向排名。
- **[Face Alignment Across Large Poses: A 3D Solution](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_cvpr_2016/html/Zhu_Face_Alignment_Across_CVPR_2016_paper.html)** — CVPR 2016 · Published · 3D. 将稠密 3D 人脸模型拟合到单张图像，并通过合成侧脸样本缓解大姿态标注问题。适合研究侧脸、自遮挡以及 3DMM 参数回归；输入 RGB 不意味着输出是 2D。
- **[How Far Are We From Solving the 2D & 3D Face Alignment Problem? (And a Dataset of 230,000 3D Facial Landmarks)](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_iccv_2017/html/Bulat_How_Far_Are_ICCV_2017_paper.html)** — ICCV 2017 · Published · 2D / 3D. FAN 基线结合关键点定位网络与残差模块，并以 2D 引导方式统一生成 LS3D-W 标注。比较大姿态、初始化、图像分辨率及模型容量对 2D/3D 对齐的影响。 [Code](https://github.com/1adrianb/face-alignment)
- **[Fine-Grained Head Pose Estimation Without Keypoints](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_cvpr_2018_workshops/w41/html/Ruiz_Fine-Grained_Head_Pose_CVPR_2018_paper.html)** — CVPR Workshops 2018 · Published · 3D. HopeNet 不依赖检测关键点，联合角度分箱分类与回归直接估计 yaw、pitch、roll。适合研究 landmark-free 头姿基线；正式发表为 workshop，不是 CVPR 主会。
- **[Look at Boundary: A Boundary-Aware Face Alignment Algorithm](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_cvpr_2018/html/Wu_Look_at_Boundary_CVPR_2018_paper.html)** — CVPR 2018 · Published · 2D. 先估计脸部边界线，再利用其几何结构定位关键点，减少关键点定义歧义。论文同时提出 WFLW，覆盖姿态、表情、光照、化妆、遮挡与模糊等挑战。 [Code](https://github.com/wywu/LAB)
- **[Wing Loss for Robust Facial Landmark Localisation With Convolutional Neural Networks](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_cvpr_2018/html/Feng_Wing_Loss_for_CVPR_2018_paper.html)** — CVPR 2018 · Published · 2D. 提出分段 Wing loss，强化小、中等定位误差的学习，并采用姿态数据均衡缓解大角度样本不足。适合研究坐标回归损失及数据分布，而非新 3D 头姿表示。
- **[Joint 3D Face Reconstruction and Dense Alignment with Position Map Regression Network](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_ECCV_2018/html/Yao_Feng_Joint_3D_Face_ECCV_2018_paper.html)** — ECCV 2018 · Published · 3D. PRNet 用 UV position map 表示完整人脸的 3D 坐标，由单张 RGB 回归稠密形状与对应关系。无需预设形变脸模型，是 UV 表示路线的重要基线。 [Code](https://github.com/YadiraF/PRNet)
- **[FSA-Net: Learning Fine-Grained Structure Aggregation for Head Pose Estimation From a Single Image](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPR_2019/html/Yang_FSA-Net_Learning_Fine-Grained_Structure_Aggregation_for_Head_Pose_Estimation_From_CVPR_2019_paper.html)** — CVPR 2019 · Published · 3D. 利用细粒度结构映射进行空间分组与特征聚合，再结合软分阶段回归直接预测头姿。适合研究紧凑 landmark-free RGB 模型与不同聚合变体的互补性。
- **[Adaptive Wing Loss for Robust Face Alignment via Heatmap Regression](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_ICCV_2019/html/Wang_Adaptive_Wing_Loss_for_Robust_Face_Alignment_via_Heatmap_Regression_ICCV_2019_paper.html)** — ICCV 2019 · Published · 2D. 针对热图前景与背景像素不均衡设计自适应损失和加权损失图，并结合边界预测。与 Wing loss 的直接坐标回归关注点不同，是热图损失设计的代表。 [Code](https://github.com/protossw512/AdaptiveWingLoss)
- **[DeCaFA: Deep Convolutional Cascade for Face Alignment in the Wild](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_ICCV_2019/papers/Dapogny_DeCaFA_Deep_Convolutional_Cascade_for_Face_Alignment_in_the_Wild_ICCV_2019_paper.pdf)** — ICCV 2019 · Published · 2D. 用全卷积级联和 landmark-wise attention 逐步细化位置，保持空间分辨率并融合各阶段特征。可在一个框架内利用不同关键点数量的异构标注。
- **[PFLD: A Practical Facial Landmark Detector](https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.10859)** — arXiv 2019 · Preprint · 2D. 面向移动端的单阶段紧凑人脸关键点模型，训练阶段利用旋转信息作几何正则，推理时不需辅助头姿分支。这里只确认预印本，不将其误标为顶会正式论文。
- **[LUVLi Face Alignment: Estimating Landmarks' Location, Uncertainty, and Visibility Likelihood](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPR_2020/html/Kumar_LUVLi_Face_Alignment_Estimating_Landmarks_Location_Uncertainty_and_Visibility_Likelihood_CVPR_2020_paper.html)** — CVPR 2020 · Published · 2D. 联合估计关键点位置、不确定性及可见性，并区分自遮挡与外部遮挡标注。适合需要筛除不可靠对齐结果的下游应用，不只关注平均定位误差。
- **[Towards Fast, Accurate and Stable 3D Dense Face Alignment](https://github.com/cleardusk/3DDFA_V2)** — ECCV 2020 · Published · 3D. 3DDFA V2 面向单目稠密对齐的速度、精度与稳定性；作者提供 PyTorch、ONNX 与视频示例。适合需要可运行工程基线的 3DMM/稠密人脸对齐研究。 [Code](https://github.com/cleardusk/3DDFA_V2)
- **[WHENet: Real-time Fine-Grained Estimation for Wide Range Head Pose](https://bmva-archive.org.uk/bmvc/2020/conference/papers/paper_0907.html)** — BMVC 2020 · Published · 3D. 面向全范围 yaw 的实时细粒度头姿估计，扩展只关注正脸附近角度的方法。适合对比 wide-range 与 front-view 评测协议。 [Code](https://github.com/Ascend-Research/HeadPoseEstimation-WHENet)
- **[img2pose: Face Alignment and Detection via 6DoF, Face Pose Estimation](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2021/html/Albiero_img2pose_Face_Alignment_and_Detection_via_6DoF_Face_Pose_Estimation_CVPR_2021_paper.html)** — CVPR 2021 · Published · 3D. 通过 6DoF 人脸姿态将检测与对齐关联，区别于只估计三轴旋转的方法。适合研究多脸定位与相机空间几何，而不是单张裁剪脸的角度回归。
- **[Pixel-in-Pixel Net: Towards Efficient Facial Landmark Detection in the Wild](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11263-021-01521-4)** — IJCV 2021 · Published · 2D. 以 pixel-in-pixel 定位头兼顾速度与精度，引入邻域约束和可泛化半监督训练处理跨域差异。按 IJCV 2021 记录，arXiv 首发为 2020。 [Code](https://github.com/jhb86253817/PIPNet)
- **[ADNet: Leveraging Error-Bias Towards Normal Direction in Face Alignment](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2021/papers/Huang_ADNet_Leveraging_Error-Bias_Towards_Normal_Direction_in_Face_Alignment_ICCV_2021_paper.pdf)** — ICCV 2021 · Published · 2D. 观察脸部边界关键点误差沿切向扩散的特点，设计各向异性方向损失和注意模块。把标注歧义与法向、切向误差区分开来建模。
- **[Revisting Quantization Error in Face Alignment](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2021W/MFR/html/Lan_Revisting_Quantization_Error_in_Face_Alignment_ICCVW_2021_paper.html)** — ICCV Workshops 2021 · Published · 2D. Heatmap-in-Heatmap 将坐标分解为两层热图，补偿下采样引起的量化误差。正式条目题名保留 proceedings 拼写 Revisting，且为 workshop。
- **[6D Rotation Representation For Unconstrained Head Pose Estimation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.12555)** — ICIP 2022 · Published · 3D. 采用连续 6D 旋转表示与测地线损失估计头部方向，避开 Euler 表示的部分不连续性。注意 6D 是旋转参数表示，不代表包含平移的 6DoF。 [Code](https://github.com/thohemp/6DRepNet)
- **[HHP-Net: A Light Heteroscedastic Neural Network for Head Pose Estimation With Uncertainty](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/WACV2022/papers/Cantarini_HHP-Net_A_Light_Heteroscedastic_Neural_Network_for_Head_Pose_Estimation_WACV_2022_paper.pdf)** — WACV 2022 · Published · 3D. 轻量异方差头姿网络，同时关注角度预测及其不确定性。适合构建风险感知头姿输出，对比只报告单一角度的回归器。
- **[LwPosr: Lightweight Efficient Fine Grained Head Pose Estimation](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/WACV2022/papers/Dhingra_LwPosr_Lightweight_Efficient_Fine_Grained_Head_Pose_Estimation_WACV_2022_paper.pdf)** — WACV 2022 · Published · 3D. 以深度可分离卷积和 Transformer encoder 组成双流多阶段回归结构。适合研究轻量头姿模型中的局部卷积与全局关系建模。
- **[Towards Accurate Facial Landmark Detection via Cascaded Transformers](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2022/papers/Li_Towards_Accurate_Facial_Landmark_Detection_via_Cascaded_Transformers_CVPR_2022_paper.pdf)** — CVPR 2022 · Published · 2D. 使用级联 Transformer 逐步提升人脸关键点定位精度，代表从卷积热图向注意力模型扩展的路线。适合与 FAN、PIPNet 的结构设计进行比较。
- **[TokenHPE: Learning Orientation Tokens for Efficient Head Pose Estimation via Transformers](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Zhang_TokenHPE_Learning_Orientation_Tokens_for_Efficient_Head_Pose_Estimation_via_CVPR_2023_paper.html)** — CVPR 2023 · Published · 3D. 利用方向 token 建模姿态相关的全局关系，面向高效 Transformer 头姿估计。适合研究 token 设计与 landmark-free 姿态回归。
- **[STAR Loss: Reducing Semantic Ambiguity in Facial Landmark Detection](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Zhou_STAR_Loss_Reducing_Semantic_Ambiguity_in_Facial_Landmark_Detection_CVPR_2023_paper.html)** — CVPR 2023 · Published · 2D. 利用预测分布的各向异性表示关键点语义歧义，自适应减轻不一致标注的影响，并用特征值约束稳定训练。可与 ADNet、Adaptive Wing 对照阅读。 [Code](https://github.com/ZhenglinZhou/STAR)
- **[Toward Robust and Unconstrained Full Range of Rotation Head Pose Estimation](https://doi.org/10.1109/TIP.2024.3378180)** — IEEE TIP 2024 · Published · 3D. 6DRepNet 的全范围旋转扩展，覆盖完整头姿方向。与 2022 ICIP 版本关联阅读，避免将期刊扩展误当成完全无关方法。 [Code](https://github.com/thohemp/6DRepNet360)

### Hand

- **[Reconstructing Hands in 3D with Transformers](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Pavlakos_Reconstructing_Hands_in_3D_with_Transformers_CVPR_2024_paper.html)** — CVPR 2024 · Published · 3D. 扩展数据和 Vision Transformer 规模实现单目手部网格恢复；HInt 提供野外 2D 关键点评测。
- **[HandDiff: 3D Hand Pose Estimation with Diffusion on Image-Point Cloud](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Cheng_HandDiff_3D_Hand_Pose_Estimation_with_Diffusion_on_Image-Point_Cloud_CVPR_2024_paper.html)** — CVPR 2024 · Published · 3D. 在图像点云条件下迭代去噪，结合逐关节与局部细节条件定位手关节。 [Code](https://github.com/cwc1260/HandDiff)
- **[EvHandPose: Event-Based 3D Hand Pose Estimation With Sparse Supervision](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.02862)** — TPAMI 2024 · Published · 3D. 事件相机手部流表示与稀疏监督，面向快速运动和高动态范围；arXiv 初稿发表于 2023 年。 [Code](https://github.com/AlanJiang98/EvHandPose)
- **[WiLoR: End-to-end 3D Hand Localization and Reconstruction in-the-wild](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/papers/Potamias_WiLoR_End-to-end_3D_Hand_Localization_and_Reconstruction_in-the-wild_CVPR_2025_paper.pdf)** — CVPR 2025 · Published · 3D. 整合实时手部定位和 Transformer 重建，面向野外多手场景；arXiv 首发于 2024 年。
- **[HandOS: 3D Hand Reconstruction in One Stage](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Chen_HandOS_3D_Hand_Reconstruction_in_One_Stage_CVPR_2025_paper.html)** — CVPR 2025 · Published · 2D / 3D. 一阶段整合检测、2D 关键点和 3D 网格，采用交互式 2D–3D 解码器。
- **[HaWoR: World-Space Hand Motion Reconstruction from Egocentric Videos](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/papers/Zhang_HaWoR_World-Space_Hand_Motion_Reconstruction_from_Egocentric_Videos_CVPR_2025_paper.pdf)** — CVPR 2025 · Published · 3D. 分解相机空间手运动与世界空间相机轨迹，并补全手部移出视野的运动。
- **[Diffusion-based 3D Hand Motion Recovery with Intuitive Physics](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/html/Zhang_Diffusion-based_3D_Hand_Motion_Recovery_with_Intuitive_Physics_ICCV_2025_paper.html)** — ICCV 2025 · Published · 3D. 使用扩散与物理约束细化逐帧手部重建，提高交互场景下的时序一致性。
- **[3D Hand Pose Estimation via Articulated Anchor-to-Joint 3D Local Regressors](https://doi.org/10.1109/TPAMI.2025.3609907)** — TPAMI 2026 · Published · 3D. 采用 articulated anchors 与局部回归，覆盖 RGB 和深度输入。2025-09-16 在线发表，2026 年卷期。 [Code](https://github.com/ChanglongJiangGit/A2J-Transformer-Plus)
- **[REACH: Hand Pose Estimation from Room Corners](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22231)** — arXiv 2026 · Preprint · 3D. 结合手身协调、时序和多视角观测，从房间角落相机估计远处低分辨率手部；正式接收状态未核实。
- **[KAD-Net: Kinematics-Aware Decoupled Learning for Robust 3D Hand Pose Estimation from a Single Depth Image](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12559)** — arXiv 2026 · Preprint · 3D. 用手指拓扑约束增强遮挡关节，并解耦 UV 定位与深度回归；正式接收状态未核实。
- **[Hand Visibility Detector: Per-Keypoint Visibility Estimation for Hands](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11574)** — arXiv 2026 · Preprint · 2D / 3D. 估计 2D 手关键点可见性，并辅助多视角 3D 三角化；属于辅助任务，而非独立 3D 重建器。
- **[Gesture-Aware Pretraining and Token Fusion for 3D Hand Pose Estimation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.17396)** — arXiv 2026 · Preprint · 3D. 手势标签预训练结合逐关节 token Transformer 回归 MANO 参数，在 InterHand2.6M 评测。
- **[Learning to Estimate 3D Hand Pose From Single RGB Images](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_iccv_2017/html/Zimmermann_Learning_to_Estimate_ICCV_2017_paper.html)** — ICCV 2017 · Published · 3D. 早期单目 RGB 到三维手关节的代表工作，分离手区域、二维关键点及三维姿态推断。适合追溯 RGB 手姿估计和 RHD 基准的发展。 [Code](https://github.com/lmb-freiburg/hand3d)
