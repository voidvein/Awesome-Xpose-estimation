## Index

### Head

- **[Mitigating Data Imbalance in Head Pose Estimation Through Diffusion Model Generated Synthetic Data with Pose Reference](https://doi.org/10.3390/app162110839)** — Applied Sciences 2026 · Published · 3D. 用姿态参考控制人脸图像生成，补充少见头部朝向，再由已有模型为合成图重新标注并训练头部旋转估计器。收益随真实测试域和训练组合变化。

- **[RRL: reciprocity relationship learning with rotation-adaptive transformers for accurate head pose estimation](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ins.2026.124033)** — Information Sciences 2027 · Published · 3D. RRL 通过自适应训练特征融合和面部结构互易性建模，利用不同脸部区域在转头时的联动线索估计三维头部朝向。
- [code](https://github.com/hnuzhy/SemiUHPE) **[Semi-Supervised Unconstrained Head Pose Estimation in the Wild](https://doi.org/10.1109/TPAMI.2025.3631642)** — TPAMI 2026 · Published · 3D. 利用未标注野外头部图像进行半监督全范围旋转估计；预印本首发于 2024 年，正式发表于 TPAMI 2026 年第 48 卷第 3 期。
- **[Coordinate Singularities Break Conformal Coverage for Gaze and Head Pose](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-032-37252-9_17)** — ECCV 2026 · Published · 3D. 校准已有视线与头姿模型的三维预测集合。以流形测地距离代替欧拉角残差，可减少高俯仰区域被整体覆盖率掩盖的漏覆盖。
- [code](https://github.com/VasilikiVas/ReVA-HPE) **[ReVA-HPE: Relative 6DoF Head Pose Estimation with Synthetic Pose Supervision and Personalized Virtual Anchors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03283)** — arXiv; accepted WACV 2027 (author-reported) 2026 · Preprint · 3D. 利用个性化虚拟参考图像进行相对 6DoF 估计，减少对真实参考图像已知姿态的依赖。
- [code](https://github.com/asirigawesha/STAGNet) **[STAG-Net: Structure-Aware Attention over Landmark Groups for Lightweight Head Pose Estimation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07828)** — arXiv 2026 · Preprint · 3D. 基于关键点分组和结构注意力进行轻量头姿回归，面向低计算成本部署。
- [code](https://github.com/VasilikiVas/vggt-hpe) **[VGGT-HPE: Reframing Head Pose Estimation as Relative Pose Prediction](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026W/ABAW/html/Vasileiou_VGGT-HPE_Reframing_Head_Pose_Estimation_as_Relative_Pose_Prediction_CVPRW_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 ABAW Workshop · Published · 3D. 将绝对头姿回归改写为相对于已知姿态参考图像的几何变换预测；以合成数据微调几何基础模型。
- [code](https://github.com/Kartik-3004/facexformer) **[FaceXFormer: A Unified Transformer for Facial Analysis](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/html/Narayan_FaceXFormer_A_Unified_Transformer_for_Facial_Analysis_ICCV_2025_paper.html)** — ICCV 2025 · Published · 2D / 3D. 统一人脸分析 Transformer，覆盖关键点、头姿、人脸解析及属性等多任务。
- [code](https://github.com/ca-joe-yang/regression-without-softarg) **[Heatmap Regression without Soft-Argmax for Facial Landmark Detection](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/html/Yang_Heatmap_Regression_without_Soft-Argmax_for_Facial_Landmark_Detection_ICCV_2025_paper.html)** — ICCV 2025 · Published · 2D. 重新审视热图关键点检测中 Soft-argmax 的必要性，以经典结构化预测目标替代该近似，在保持热图建模的同时改变训练方式。
- **[PossLoss: A Reliable and Sensitive Facial Landmark Detection Loss Function](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/html/Zhu_PossLoss_A_Reliable_and_Sensitive_Facial_Landmark_Detection_Loss_Function_ICCV_2025_paper.html)** — ICCV 2025 · Published · 2D. PossLoss 面向关键点热图的前景背景不均衡与峰值偏差，采用自加权和样本敏感机制，将学习重点自适应分配到较难定位的关键点。
- [code](https://github.com/phflot/tfake) **[T-FAKE: Synthesizing Thermal Images for Facial Landmarking](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Flotho_T-FAKE_Synthesizing_Thermal_Images_for_Facial_Landmarking_CVPR_2025_paper.html)** — CVPR 2025 · Published · 2D. T-FAKE 使用 RGB2Thermal 损失合成带稀疏与稠密关键点的热红外人脸，结合概率关键点预测和标注适配，面向不同标注规范的热成像定位。
- [code](https://github.com/touranisatyajit/cvpr2025-uld-headpose) **[Unsupervised Discovery of Facial Landmarks and Head Pose](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Tourani_Unsupervised_Discovery_of_Facial_Landmarks_and_Head_Pose_CVPR_2025_paper.html)** — CVPR 2025 · Published · 2D / 3D. 使用 Stable Diffusion 特征联合发现人脸关键点与头姿，通过几何约束相互优化。
- [code](https://github.com/ZhimingZo/unsup-face-analysis) **[Towards unsupervised learning of joint facial landmark detection and head pose estimation](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0031320325000536)** — Pattern Recognition 2025 · Published · 2D / 3D. 通过可学习的标准姿态 3D 关键点与图像重建联合学习关键点和刚体头姿。
- [code](https://github.com/Human3DAIGC/AccurateFacialLandmarkDetection) **[Cascaded Dual Vision Transformer for Accurate Facial Landmark Detection](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/WACV2025/html/Dang_Cascaded_Dual_Vision_Transformer_for_Accurate_Facial_Landmark_Detection_WACV_2025_paper.html)** — WACV 2025 · Published · 2D. 将通道分割 Transformer 与空间分割 Transformer 结合，以建模关键点间几何关系；长跳跃连接把低层图像特征传递到各级预测模块。
- [code](https://github.com/ben0919/ORFormer) **[ORFormer: Occlusion-Robust Transformer for Accurate Facial Landmark Detection](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/WACV2025/html/Chiang_ORFormer_Occlusion-Robust_Transformer_for_Accurate_Facial_Landmark_Detection_WACV_2025_paper.html)** — WACV 2025 · Published · 2D. ORFormer 为图块配置额外 messenger token，从其他图块聚合信息；通过局部与外部表示的一致性识别不可见区域，并恢复缺失特征用于热图预测。
- **[FaceLift: Semi-supervised 3D Facial Landmark Localization](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Ferman_FaceLift_Semi-supervised_3D_Facial_Landmark_Localization_CVPR_2024_paper.html)** — CVPR 2024 · Published · 3D. 以半监督学习定位 3D 人脸关键点，关注人工定义与 3DMM 注册标注的一致性。
- [code](https://github.com/plustwo0/generalized-face-landmarker) **[Generalizable Face Landmarking Guided by Conditional Face Warping](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Liang_Generalizable_Face_Landmarking_Guided_by_Conditional_Face_Warping_CVPR_2024_paper.html)** — CVPR 2024 · Published · 2D. 将关键点预测嵌入条件人脸变形器，结合带标注真实脸与无标注风格化人脸生成伪关键点，交替优化以提高跨风格泛化。
- **[HeadDiff: Exploring Rotation Uncertainty With Diffusion Models for Head Pose Estimation](https://doi.org/10.1109/TIP.2024.3372457)** — IEEE TIP 2024 · Published · 3D. 采用概率扩散回归建模野外图像中的头部旋转不确定性。
- [code](https://github.com/thohemp/6DRepNet360) **[Toward Robust and Unconstrained Full Range of Rotation Head Pose Estimation](https://doi.org/10.1109/TIP.2024.3378180)** — IEEE TIP 2024 · Published · 3D. 6DRepNet 的全范围旋转扩展，覆盖完整头姿方向。与 2022 ICIP 版本关联阅读，避免将期刊扩展误当成完全无关方法。
- [code](https://github.com/asw91666/TRG-Release) **[6DoF Head Pose Estimation through Explicit Bidirectional Interaction with Face Geometry](https://www.ecva.net/papers/eccv_2024/papers_ECCV/html/4824_ECCV_2024_paper.php)** — ECCV 2024 · Published · 3D. 通过脸部几何与姿态的双向交互估计 6DoF 头姿，特别关注平移估计。
- **[Infinite 3D Landmarks: Improving Continuous 2D Facial Landmark Detection](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/cgf.15126)** — Computer Graphics Forum 2024 · Published · 2D / 3D. 借助 3D 几何改进连续 2D 人脸关键点检测。
- [code](https://github.com/pcr-upm/opal23_headpose) **[On the representation and methodology for wide and short range head pose estimation](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0031320324000141)** — Pattern Recognition 2024 · Published · 3D. 研究不同角度范围的头姿表示和评测，并提供基于 CMU Panoptic 的广角度基准。
- **[Deep learning and machine learning techniques for head pose estimation: a survey](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10462-024-10936-7)** — Artificial Intelligence Review 2024 · Published · 3D. 为头姿估计的方法比较与后续研究提供较新的综述入口。
- [code](https://github.com/libingzeng/landmark_consistent_plugin) **[3D-Aware Facial Landmark Detection via Multi-View Consistent Training on Synthetic Data](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Zeng_3D-Aware_Facial_Landmark_Detection_via_Multi-View_Consistent_Training_on_Synthetic_CVPR_2023_paper.html)** — CVPR 2023 · Published · 2D / 3D. 利用生成模型与神经渲染合成多视图训练数据，以多视图一致性约束增强二维、三维人脸关键点预测的三维几何一致性。
- [code](https://github.com/lhyfst/DSFNet) **[DSFNet: Dual Space Fusion Network for Occlusion-Robust 3D Dense Face Alignment](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Li_DSFNet_Dual_Space_Fusion_Network_for_Occlusion-Robust_3D_Dense_Face_CVPR_2023_paper.html)** — CVPR 2023 · Published · 3D. DSFNet 先在图像空间预测可见区域的稠密特征，再借助三维可变形模型补全不可见部分；双空间融合兼顾遮挡、大姿态和噪声。
- [code](https://github.com/ZhenglinZhou/STAR) **[STAR Loss: Reducing Semantic Ambiguity in Facial Landmark Detection](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Zhou_STAR_Loss_Reducing_Semantic_Ambiguity_in_Facial_Landmark_Detection_CVPR_2023_paper.html)** — CVPR 2023 · Published · 2D. 利用预测分布的各向异性表示关键点语义歧义，自适应减轻不一致标注的影响，并用特征值约束稳定训练。可与 ADNet、Adaptive Wing 对照阅读。
- [code](https://github.com/zc2023/TokenHPE) **[TokenHPE: Learning Orientation Tokens for Efficient Head Pose Estimation via Transformers](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Zhang_TokenHPE_Learning_Orientation_Tokens_for_Efficient_Head_Pose_Estimation_via_CVPR_2023_paper.html)** — CVPR 2023 · Published · 3D. 利用方向 token 建模姿态相关的全局关系，面向高效 Transformer 头姿估计。适合研究 token 设计与 landmark-free 姿态回归。
- **[Deep Learning for Head Pose Estimation: A Survey](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s42979-023-01796-z)** — SN Computer Science 2023 · Published · 3D. 提供从传统路线到深度模型的头姿估计研究地图，适合定位数据及方法选择。
- [code](https://github.com/PinataFarms/DAD-3DHeads) **[DAD-3DHeads: A Large-Scale Dense, Accurate and Diverse Dataset for 3D Head Alignment From a Single Image](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2022/html/Martyniuk_DAD-3DHeads_A_Large-Scale_Dense_Accurate_and_Diverse_Dataset_for_3D_CVPR_2022_paper.html)** — CVPR 2022 · Published · 3D. 提供多样化头部数据集，每个头部提供 3,500 余个稠密关键点标注，并训练 DAD-3DNet 联合预测 FLAME 形状、表情与姿态，以评估自然场景三维头部对齐和域偏移鲁棒性。
- [code](https://github.com/zhuccly/GlomFace-Face-Alignment) **[Occlusion-Robust Face Alignment Using a Viewpoint-Invariant Hierarchical Network Architecture](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2022/html/Zhu_Occlusion-Robust_Face_Alignment_Using_a_Viewpoint-Invariant_Hierarchical_Network_Architecture_CVPR_2022_paper.html)** — CVPR 2022 · Published · 2D. - [code](https://github.com/Jiahao-UTS/SLPT-master) **[Sparse Local Patch Transformer for Robust Face Alignment and Landmarks Inherent Relation Learning](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2022/html/Xia_Sparse_Local_Patch_Transformer_for_Robust_Face_Alignment_and_Landmarks_CVPR_2022_paper.html)** — CVPR 2022 · Published · 2D. SLPT 从各关键点周围的稀疏局部图块提取表示，以注意力学习点间关系，再由粗到细调整图块尺度和亚像素坐标。
- **[Towards Accurate Facial Landmark Detection via Cascaded Transformers](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2022/papers/Li_Towards_Accurate_Facial_Landmark_Detection_via_Cascaded_Transformers_CVPR_2022_paper.pdf)** — CVPR 2022 · Published · 2D. 使用级联 Transformer 逐步提升人脸关键点定位精度，代表从卷积热图向注意力模型扩展的路线。适合与 FAN、PIPNet 的结构设计进行比较。
- [code](https://github.com/thohemp/6DRepNet) **[6D Rotation Representation For Unconstrained Head Pose Estimation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.12555)** — ICIP 2022 · Published · 3D. 采用连续 6D 旋转表示与测地线损失估计头部方向，避开 Euler 表示的部分不连续性。注意 6D 是旋转参数表示，不代表包含平移的 6DoF。
- [code](https://github.com/cantarinigiorgio/HHP-Net) **[HHP-Net: A Light Heteroscedastic Neural Network for Head Pose Estimation With Uncertainty](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/WACV2022/papers/Cantarini_HHP-Net_A_Light_Heteroscedastic_Neural_Network_for_Head_Pose_Estimation_WACV_2022_paper.pdf)** — WACV 2022 · Published · 3D. 轻量异方差头姿网络，同时关注角度预测及其不确定性。适合构建风险感知头姿输出，对比只报告单一角度的回归器。
- **[LwPosr: Lightweight Efficient Fine Grained Head Pose Estimation](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/WACV2022/papers/Dhingra_LwPosr_Lightweight_Efficient_Fine_Grained_Head_Pose_Estimation_WACV_2022_paper.pdf)** — WACV 2022 · Published · 3D. 以深度可分离卷积和 Transformer encoder 组成双流多阶段回归结构。适合研究轻量头姿模型中的局部卷积与全局关系建模。
- [code](https://github.com/huangyangyu/ADNet) **[ADNet: Leveraging Error-Bias Towards Normal Direction in Face Alignment](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2021/papers/Huang_ADNet_Leveraging_Error-Bias_Towards_Normal_Direction_in_Face_Alignment_ICCV_2021_paper.pdf)** — ICCV 2021 · Published · 2D. 观察脸部边界关键点误差沿切向扩散的特点，设计各向异性方向损失和注意模块。把标注歧义与法向、切向误差区分开来建模。
- [code](https://github.com/zhuccly/SAAT) **[Improving Robustness of Facial Landmark Detection by Defending Against Adversarial Attacks](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2021/html/Zhu_Improving_Robustness_of_Facial_Landmark_Detection_by_Defending_Against_Adversarial_ICCV_2021_paper.html)** — ICCV 2021 · Published · 2D.
